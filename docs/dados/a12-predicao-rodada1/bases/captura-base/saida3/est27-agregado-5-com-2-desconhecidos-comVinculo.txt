## Fórmulas de Síntese BOCR Validadas (RAG)

### Additive method to synthesize BOCR priorities. (alizadeh2020_energy, 2020)
    $$
    P_i = bB_i + oO_i + c(1/C_i)_{\text{Normalized}} + r(1/R_i)_{\text{Normalized}}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B_i=Benefits score, O_i=Opportunities score, C_i=Costs score, R_i=Risks score, b=Benefit weight, o=Opportunity weight, c=Cost weight, r=Risk weight
    *Condições:* The additive formula is the best for long-term results.

### Probabilistic additive method to synthesize BOCR priorities. (alizadeh2020_energy, 2020)
    $$
    P_i = bB_i + oO_i + c(1-C_i) + r(1-R_i)
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B_i=Benefits, O_i=Opportunities, C_i=Costs, R_i=Risks, b=Benefit weight, o=Opportunity weight, c=Cost weight, r=Risk weight
    *Condições:* Gerais

### Subtractive method to synthesize BOCR priorities. (alizadeh2020_energy, 2020)
    $$
    P_i = bB_i + oO_i - cC_i - rR_i
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B_i=Benefits, O_i=Opportunities, C_i=Costs, R_i=Risks, b=Benefit weight, o=Opportunity weight, c=Cost weight, r=Risk weight
    *Condições:* Gerais

### Multiplicative priority powers method to synthesize BOCR priorities. (alizadeh2020_energy, 2020)
    $$
    P_i = B_i^b O_i^o [(1/C_i)_{\text{Normalized}}]^c [(1/R_i)_{\text{Normalized}}]^r
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B_i=Benefits, O_i=Opportunities, C_i=Costs, R_i=Risks, b=Benefit weight, o=Opportunity weight, c=Cost weight, r=Risk weight
    *Condições:* Gerais

### Multiplicative ratio method to synthesize BOCR priorities. (alizadeh2020_energy, 2020)
    $$
    P_i = \frac{B_i O_i}{C_i R_i}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B_i=Benefits, O_i=Opportunities, C_i=Costs, R_i=Risks
    *Condições:* multiplicative formula is equivalent to the marginal cost/benefit and the best for short-term results (BO/CR).

### Computes the overall score of each supplier using an additive formulation for BOCR merits, considering Costs and Risks as inverted. (demirtas2008_integrated, 2008)
    $$
    W_{i} = R_{b}*W_{ib} + R_{o}*W_{io} + R_{c}*(1-W_{ic}) + R_{r}*(1-W_{ir})
    $$
    *Variáveis:* W_i=overall score of the ith supplier, W_{ib}, W_{io}, W_{ic}, W_{ir}=total weights of the ith supplier according to Benefits, Opportunities, Costs, and Risks, R_b, R_o, R_c, R_r=rating results (weights) of the BOCR strategic criteria
    *Condições:* Used to compute the overall score to rank alternatives and serve as objective function coefficients.

### Computes the overall score using a negative additive (subtractive) formulation for Costs and Risks. (demirtas2008_integrated, 2008)
    $$
    W_{i} = R_{b}*W_{ib} + R_{o}*W_{io} - R_{c}*W_{ic} - R_{r}*W_{ir}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* W_i=overall score of the ith supplier, W_{ib}, W_{io}, W_{ic}, W_{ir}=total weights of the ith supplier according to Benefits, Opportunities, Costs, and Risks, R_b, R_o, R_c, R_r=rating results (weights) of the BOCR strategic criteria
    *Condições:* Alternative to Eq. 2; its use may change the final rank of the alternatives.

    $$
    *Variáveis:* P_i=overall priority of alternative i
    *Condições:* Ratio of the product of positive merits to the product of negative merits. Does not explicitly use merit weights b, o, c, r in the final formula shown.

### Basic BOCR additive formula with negative criteria subtracted. (liang2022_ahp_undesirable, 2022)
    $$
    bB+oO-cC-rR = \text{value for alternative}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* b, o, c, r=Weight coefficients of benefit, opportunities, costs, and risks criteria, B, O, C, R=Values of the alternative for each respective criterion
    *Condições:* Values of criteria for each alternative must be normalized so 1 is best for positive and -1 is worst for negative.

### Multiplicative combination of BOCR priorities. (liang2022_ahp_undesirable, 2022)
    $$
    BO/CR
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B, O, C, R=Synthesized priorities for Benefits, Opportunities, Costs, and Risks
    *Condições:* One of the four ways to combine the four criteria to yield good results.

### Additive combination where negative priorities are transformed using reciprocal values. (liang2022_ahp_undesirable, 2022)
    $$
    bB+oO+c(1/C)+r(1/R)
    $$
    *Variáveis:* b, o, c, r=Weights for B, O, C, R, B, O, C, R=Synthesized priorities for Benefits, Opportunities, Costs, and Risks
    *Condições:* One of the four ways to combine the four criteria to yield good results.

### Additive combination where negative priorities are transformed using complement (1 - value). (liang2022_ahp_undesirable, 2022)
    $$
    bB+oO+c(1-C)+r(1-R)
    $$
    *Variáveis:* b, o, c, r=Weights for B, O, C, R, B, O, C, R=Synthesized priorities for Benefits, Opportunities, Costs, and Risks
    *Condições:* One of the four ways to combine the four criteria to yield good results.

### Standard multiplicative BOCR ratio analysis. (mu2016_bocr, 2016)
    $$
    (B \times O) / (C \times R)
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B=Benefits, O=Opportunities, C=Costs, R=Risks
    *Condições:* Intuitive simplicity; used when factors are mostly intangible rather than monetary.

### Additive with subtraction formula for BOCR. (mu2016_bocr, 2016)
    $$
    B + O - C - R
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B=Benefits, O=Opportunities, C=Costs, R=Risks
    *Condições:* Extension of the original net value approach (B-C).

### Additive synthesis method for BOCR containing only positive priorities. (petrillo2023state, 2023)
    $$
    x = Bb + Oo + Cc + Rr
    $$
    *Variáveis:* x=overall priority, B, O, C, R=weights of each BOCR criterion, b, o, c, r=priorities of the alternative according to each criterion
    *Condições:* Only in Equation (1) are there positive priorities.

### Additive synthesis method for BOCR using reciprocals for negative priorities (costs and risks). (petrillo2023state, 2023)
    $$
    x = Bb + Oo + C(1/c) + R(1/r)
    $$
    *Variáveis:* x=overall priority, B, O, C, R=weights of each BOCR criterion, b, o, c, r=priorities of the alternative according to each criterion
    *Condições:* Equation (2) has negative priorities obtained with comparison matrices.

### Additive synthesis method for BOCR using complementary/residual values for negative priorities. (petrillo2023state, 2023)
    $$
    x = Bb + Oo + C(1-c) + R(1-r)
    $$
    *Variáveis:* x=overall priority, B, O, C, R=weights of each BOCR criterion, b, o, c, r=priorities of the alternative according to each criterion
    *Condições:* Equation (3) deals with the concept of residual or complimentary values.

### Multiplicative synthesis method (tradeoff) for BOCR priorities. (petrillo2023state, 2023)
    $$
    x = \frac{b^{B}o^{O}}{c^{C}r^{R}}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* x=overall priority, B, O, C, R=weights of each BOCR criterion, b, o, c, r=priorities of the alternative according to each criterion
    *Condições:* Equation (4) highlights a tradeoff between benefits and costs and costs and risks.

### Multiplicative synthesis of benefits/opportunities over costs/risks (return on investment ratio). (saatyOzdemir2003_negative, 2003)
    $$
    \frac{B^{w_b} \cdot O^{w_o}}{C^{w_c} \cdot R^{w_r}}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B, O, C, R=Normalized priorities of an alternative on benefits, opportunities, costs, and risks, w_b, w_o, w_c, w_r=Strategic weights for the four BOCR factors
    *Condições:* Yields a return-on-investment ratio. Reported alongside three other synthesis methods in Table 5.

### Additive synthesis using reciprocals of costs and risks (i.e., 1/C and 1/R) to convert disadvantageous criteria into beneficial. (saatyOzdemir2003_negative, 2003)
    $$
    b \cdot B + o \cdot O + c/C + r/R
    $$
    *Variáveis:* b, o, c, r=Normalized weights of the BOCR factors, B, O, C, R=Normalized priorities of an alternative on each factor, 1/C, 1/R=Reciprocals of cost and risk priorities
    *Condições:* One of four synthesis methods compared. Criticized in Wijnmalen (2007) for distorting the original common scale.

### Additive Residual synthesis: costs and risks are converted to (1-C) and (1-R), so higher cost reduces the score additively. (saatyOzdemir2003_negative, 2003)
    $$
    b \cdot B + o \cdot O + c \cdot (1-C) + r \cdot (1-R)
    $$
    *Variáveis:* b, o, c, r=Normalized weights of BOCR factors, B, O, C, R=Normalized priorities of alternative on each factor, (1-C), (1-R)=Residual transformation: complement of normalized cost/risk relative to the largest reciprocal
    *Condições:* One of four synthesis methods presented. The residual form (1-C) yields values between 0 and 1.

### Additive Subtractive synthesis: costs and risks are subtracted directly, allowing the score to be negative when an alternative is unprofitable. (saatyOzdemir2003_negative, 2003)
    $$
    b \cdot B + o \cdot O - c \cdot C - r \cdot R
    $$
    *Variáveis:* b, o, c, r=Normalized weights of BOCR factors, B, O, C, R=Normalized priorities of alternative on each factor
    *Condições:* Result can be negative, indicating unprofitability. Approximates net monetary value better than reciprocals.

### Original multiplicative synthesis of composite priorities with weights as powers, proposed by Saaty. (wijnmalen2007_bocr, 2007)
    $$
    \frac{B_{p}^{w_{b}} * O_{p}^{w_{o}}}{C_{p}^{w_{c}} * R_{p}^{w_{r}}}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* B_{p}, O_{p}, C_{p}, R_{p}=Normalized overall priorities of alternatives on benefits, opportunities, costs, and risks, w_{b}, w_{o}, w_{c}, w_{r}=Normalized weights for each of the four factors
    *Condições:* Criticized in the paper for yielding ambiguous profitability results unless specific commensurability conditions are met.

### Additive synthesis with weights as coefficients, using reciprocals for costs and risks (approximation of multiplicative synthesis). (wijnmalen2007_bocr, 2007)
    $$
    w_{b}*B_{p} + w_{o}*O_{p} + w_{c}*1/C_{p}^{*} + w_{r}*1/R_{p}^{*}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* 1/C_{p}^{*}, 1/R_{p}^{*}=Normalized reciprocals of cost and risk priorities
    *Condições:* Criticized for distorting the scale and lacking a break-even based profitability indicator.

### Additive synthesis treating costs and risks as negative values instead of reciprocals. (wijnmalen2007_bocr, 2007)
    $$
    w_{b}*B_{p} + w_{o}*O_{p} - w_{c}*C_{p} - w_{r}*R_{p}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* 
    *Condições:* Closer representation of net monetary value, but requires commensurate priorities to ensure valid results.

### Revised synthesis using a quotient of sums of rescaled priorities to maintain consistent units. (wijnmalen2007_bocr, 2007)
    $$
    \frac{(s_{b}*B_{p}^{i})+(s_{o}*O_{p}^{i})}{(s_{c}*C_{p}^{i})+(s_{r}*R_{p}^{i})}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* s_{b}, s_{o}, s_{c}, s_{r}=Rescaling weights proportionate to the relative magnitudes (totals) of the factors
    *Condições:* Recommended. Requires priorities to be rescaled to be commensurate prior to synthesis.

### Net value oriented analysis incorporating both rescaling weights (s) for commensurability and personal evaluation weights (v). (wijnmalen2007_bocr, 2007)
    $$
    v_{b}*s_{b}*B_{p}^{i} + v_{o}*s_{o}*O_{p}^{i} - v_{c}*s_{c}*C_{p}^{i} - v_{r}*s_{r}*R_{p}^{i}
    $$
    *Variáveis:* v_{b}, v_{o}, v_{c}, v_{r}=Weights based on personal values (relative importance of factors), s_{b}, s_{o}, s_{c}, s_{r}=Magnitude-based rescaling weights
    *Condições:* Recommended for net-value oriented BOCR analysis.
